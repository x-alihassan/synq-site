SYNQ is founder-led. Ali Hassan, a Workforce Management (WFM) Lead, has spent 2 years in workforce management and the BPO industry: forecasting, scheduling, real-time management and intraday decisions in customer-service operations.

That work comes down to one question, asked every hour: who should handle this work, right now, so it gets done well and at a sensible cost? For most of that history, the answer was always a person. Now some of the work can go to AI, some should stay with people, and most teams have no clear way to decide which.

SYNQ starts with that decision, one workflow at a time.

What we believe

Five working beliefs.

  1. Completed work is the measure. Not replies or deflections, and not “automation potential.”
  2. AI is a worker with limits. It gets permissions it has earned, and it is checked like anyone else.
  3. People stay central. The cases that need judgement should go to people who have time for them.
  4. Evidence before autonomy. Historical, then shadow, then assisted, then autonomous.
  5. Honest numbers. We report cost per accepted outcome, including the cases that went wrong.

Where we are going

Service today. Vision later.

Today: we are a service. One workflow, delivered and measured. Our job is to run your workflow well.

Vision Each pilot teaches us more about how work should be split between people, AI and other providers. Over time, that knowledge becomes software. That is a direction, not something you can buy today.
