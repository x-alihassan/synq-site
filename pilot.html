The outcome

A return counts only when it is finished.

A policy-eligible return is accepted only after a return record and pickup are created, verified and audited. A recommendation, or a request for human approval, is not a completed outcome. You and SYNQ agree the exact outcome and its exclusions before stage 1.

Scope

Small on purpose.

  • One workflowFor example returns and exchanges, over chat or email.
  • One channelWhere possible.
  • One system integrationOr controlled exports if there is no API.
  • A defined volume capAgreed with you in advance.
  • Written rulesBusiness rules and permission limits, signed off by you.
  • Your team as fallbackA named human owner for anything the AI should not handle.

Your systems, your support team and your approval authority stay where they are.

The 30 days

Autonomy is earned, one gate at a time.

Four lanes of evidence converge into one path. Nothing moves to the next stage until the gate is passed.

1. Historical simulation1–7Replay anonymized cases against signed policy. Compare intent, decisions, escalation and estimated cost.Approve policy and error review.

What the pilot measures

Measured, not promised.

We establish the baseline, sample size, data access, privacy handling, volume cap and stop conditions together before launch. These are what the pilot measures, not results we promise in advance.

  • Outcome success rate
  • Automation rate
  • Human-intervention rate and reasons
  • Processing time
  • QA and accuracy
  • Cost per accepted outcome
  • Escalation reasons
  • Customer experience, where you can share it
  • Agent experience, where you can share it

What a successful pilot looks like

Eight criteria, reviewed together.

  1. You agree the outcome definition.
  2. SYNQ handles real or production-like workload.
  3. Correct cases are automated or assisted.
  4. Unsafe cases route to humans.
  5. No material policy violations occur.
  6. Cost is measurable, including your human and support time where available.
  7. You see a credible ROI path from your baseline and the actual pilot data.
  8. You ask for more volume, another workflow, or ongoing usage.

If all you can say at the end is “nice demo,” the pilot did not work. We will tell you that ourselves.
