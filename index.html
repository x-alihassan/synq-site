What this model ignores

The model is for intuition, not for building a roster. It leaves out:

  • Chat concurrency. It models one chat at a time, which is conservative.
  • Abandonment and retrials.
  • Multi-skill routing.
  • Intraday re-forecasting.
  • Breaks inside shifts.
  • Backlog carrying over from one interval to the next.

These are real effects. The pilot is where they get measured on your data.

QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

Is this real AI?
No, not the simulator. It runs fixed rules in your browser so you can see the decision logic. In a pilot, a language model only classifies the request. Your policy, in code, makes the decision.
Where do the numbers come from?
The charts and calculator are an illustrative model of a fictional desk, built on Erlang C and stated assumptions. Your numbers will differ. Nothing here is a customer result. The demo thresholds are a fictional policy.
Do we need to change our helpdesk or ecommerce platform?
No. We connect to what you already use. If a system has no API, we use exports during the pilot and keep a human step. We won't pretend a step is automated when it isn't.
Do you replace our agents?
No. Your team stays. They are the fallback, and they approve sensitive actions. We don't hire agents or supply seats.
Is this a chatbot?
No. A chatbot answers questions. We complete a defined workflow, such as a return created and a pickup booked, and measure whether it was done correctly.
What counts as a successful outcome?
We agree it in writing before the pilot. In general: the task is complete, your policy was followed, nothing invalid was done, and the case was escalated if it needed to be. If the AI says "done" and it isn't, it doesn't count.
What happens when the AI isn't sure?
The case goes to your team with the context already gathered. Unclear policy, possible fraud and VIP cases go to a person by rule.
Which AI do you use?
Established commercial models via API. The model is one part of the system. Your rules, permissions and verification are the rest.
What data do you need, and how is it handled?
For the historical test, a sample of past cases (anonymised where possible) plus your policies. In a pilot, access is limited to the one workflow and every action is logged. We'll go through your security requirements on the first call.
How much does it cost?
Pilots are scoped and priced per workflow. We propose a structure after discovery. See the pilot offer.
What about Arabic?
We look at your language mix in discovery. We only commit to the languages and channels we have tested on your historical cases.
We run a BPO. Is this relevant?
Possibly. Tell us about one workflow you run for a client.
